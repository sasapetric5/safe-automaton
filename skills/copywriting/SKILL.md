# Copywriting Skill

## Goal

Create clear, persuasive copy that matches the audience’s real problem and the product’s verified capabilities. Clarity and trust outrank hype.

## Workflow

1. **Collect inputs.** Identify page type, target audience, desired action, language, tone, constraints, and any existing copy. Ask one focused question only when a missing fact prevents a useful draft.
2. **Build a fact sheet.** Separate verified product facts, user-provided claims, reasonable hypotheses, and unknowns. Never invent customer counts, testimonials, certifications, savings, performance metrics, integrations, guarantees, or case studies.
3. **Clarify positioning.** State who the product helps, what problem it solves, how it works, and what differentiates it—only to the extent supported by evidence.
4. **Draft to the page’s job.** Use a specific headline, explanatory subheadline, concrete benefits, credible proof, clear CTA, and relevant objection handling. Match the length and structure to the page instead of adding filler.
5. **Prefer concrete language.** Replace vague superlatives with observable details. Explain limitations where they materially affect a buyer’s decision. Do not imply an AI agent has performed a task when it only generated a template or recommendation.
6. **Generate alternatives.** When useful, provide 3 distinct headline/CTA options with the rationale and trade-offs, not cosmetic paraphrases.
7. **Self-review.** Check factual accuracy, clarity, audience fit, specificity, tone, accessibility, jargon, consistency, and whether each claim can be substantiated.
8. **Deliver as a draft.** Include the final copy and a short verification checklist for facts that still require owner approval.

## Output format

- Audience and conversion goal
- Verified facts / unknowns
- Recommended draft
- Optional alternative headlines and CTAs
- Claims requiring confirmation
- QA checklist

## Zero-cost and safety constraints

No paid copy tools or APIs. Do not create fake testimonials, fake reviews, fake urgency, deceptive scarcity, hidden terms, or unsupported outcome guarantees. Do not publish, send, or deploy the copy; provide a draft for explicit review.
