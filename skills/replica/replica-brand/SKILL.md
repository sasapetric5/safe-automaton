# Brand and copy
1. Identify audience, problem, deliverable, constraints and next step.
2. Separate verified capability, user claims, hypotheses and unknowns.
3. Never invent clients, reviews, credentials, metrics, case studies or guarantees.
4. Explain inclusions, exclusions, evidence method and deliverables.
5. Do not imply work was performed when only a template was generated.
6. Review clarity, accessibility, privacy and substantiation.
7. Save an unsent draft; do not contact prospects automatically.
